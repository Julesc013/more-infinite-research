This update restores the MIR 4-style Research Library and fixes finite research-speed compatibility.

- Fixed tabs, labelled filters and clearer Research, Settings and help pages.
- Preserved selection and scrolling; fixed narrow-window resizing.
- Continue research speed from level 8 when another mod supplies finite level 7, preserving earned bonuses and unfinished work.
- Show numeric research caps in native descriptions.
- Restored missing setting names, help and dropdown translations.

[Installation, compatibility and upgrade guidance](https://github.com/Julesc013/more-infinite-research/blob/v5.0.8/docs/support.md).
