MIR 5.0.20
Channel: GitHub prerelease (beta). Mod Portal publication remains with the maintainer for stable releases.

MIR 5.0.20 fixes Research Library reflow and worker-cargo upgrade failures in the supported Space Age and Space Exploration configurations.

- On Factorio 2.0.77 and 2.1.24, the open Library adjusts to changes in GUI scale or display resolution while keeping the visible research results and selection.
- On Factorio 2.1.23 with the unchanged official Space Age mods and default worker cargo settings, the 4.7.3 upgrade preserves earned cargo, current research, repeated queue entries and parked research. The next level keeps its six-pack price and cargo reward.
- On Factorio 2.0.77 with Space Exploration 0.7.57, the upgrade from 4.7.2 restores the conquest-force policy and supports the unchanged Elevated Rails selection with the seven-pack worker contract and cap ten.

Keep the original save. For the first upgrade, sync a copy with its exact mod versions and startup settings, then change only MIR.

Broader Library interaction, mod-profile and performance qualification remain open for stable.

See the [support guide](https://github.com/Julesc013/more-infinite-research/blob/v5.0.20/docs/support.md) for installation and supported editions.
