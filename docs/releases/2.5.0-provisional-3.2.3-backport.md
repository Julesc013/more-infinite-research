## Projection decisions

- Retain the C21 concrete planet and space-location resolver correction already present on the 2.0 line.
- Retain the C24 Py finalizer-ordering correction and requalify it on the exact Factorio 2.0 closure.
- Omit the version-specific 3.2.0/3.2.1-to-3.2.2 affected-save repair because no released 2.x package created that state.
- Port the C30 Ice and Platform progression correction, with Platform using space and cryogenic science on Factorio 2.0 and no metallurgic science.
- Port the structural belt, inserter, and module discovery logic, including the automatic-action guard and one-pass indexes.
- Exercise AdvancedBeltsSA 2.3.3 natively on Factorio 2.0.77 without treating any Factorio 2.1 adapter result as target evidence.
- Retain Factorio 2.0 target profiles, dependencies, capability cuts, fixtures, and independently generated evidence.
