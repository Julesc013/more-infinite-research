## Productivity contract

| Technology | Exact recipes | Per-level effect | Science addition |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Breeding productivity | `pentapod-egg` and the established breeding set | `+10%` | Agricultural and cryogenic |
| Nutrients productivity | Yumako mash, bioflux, and biter egg nutrient recipes | `+10%` | Agricultural and cryogenic |
| Capture bot rocket productivity | `capture-robot-rocket` | `+10%` | Military and agricultural |
| Landfill productivity | `landfill`; `foundation`; `ice-platform`; `space-platform-foundation` | `+10%`; `+5%`; `+2%`; `+1%` | Existing stream selection |

The Nutrients stream excludes `nutrients-from-spoilage` because it participates in a nutrient/spoilage recovery loop. It also excludes `nutrients-from-fish`, which upstream marks as not productivity-capable, and does not broadly claim third-party nutrient recipes.

Space Age omits `allow_productivity` on the capture rocket and platform recipes. MIR grants that permission only to the three exact recipe IDs while Space Age is active, before compiler fact capture. This makes the technology effects valid and also permits productivity modules in those exact recipes.

Factorio already provides an infinite native follower-robot continuation at `follower-robot-count-5`. MIR does not duplicate it. The generation-integrity fixture requires that it remain infinite, retain one `+25` maximum-following-robots effect per level, use `1000*(L-4)` cost scaling, and keep all seven base science packs.

## Immediate playtest

1. Confirm Nutrients productivity and Capture bot rocket productivity are enabled by default and appear only with Space Age.
2. Inspect Breeding productivity and require the Pentapod egg recipe to receive exactly `+10%` per level.
3. Inspect Nutrients productivity and require exactly the three forward nutrient routes, with no spoilage or fish route.
4. Inspect Capture bot rocket productivity and require one exact recipe at `+10%` per level.
5. Inspect Landfill productivity and require landfill `+10%`, foundation `+5%`, ice platform `+2%`, and space platform foundation `+1%` per level.
6. Confirm nutrient science includes agricultural and cryogenic packs and capture rocket science includes military and agricultural packs.
7. With BZ and AAI, check owner singularity and ensure no third-party recovery, recycling, catalyst, or byproduct route was claimed.
8. Save, reload, and inspect the technology tree, tooltips, icons, prerequisites, accepting labs, and current research retention.
9. Confirm `follower-robot-count-5` remains the sole infinite follower-count continuation and advances by 25 robots per level.

Record any tuning or promotion changes before the next freeze. Any packaged adjustment after C18 creates a later candidate and requires fresh candidate-bound evidence.
