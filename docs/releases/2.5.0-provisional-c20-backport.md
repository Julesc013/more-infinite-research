The projection is reconstructed from immutable 2.4.9 history and contains published tag `3.2.0` in its ancestry through a reviewed lineage join. This preserves Factorio 2.0 history while making the semantic source and adapter surface explicit.

## Exact Target Adaptation

The projected package differs from C20 in exactly four paths:

- `README.md` supplies Factorio 2.0 player guidance.
- `changelog.txt` identifies the provisional 2.5 line.
- `info.json` declares MIR 2.5.0 and Factorio 2.0 dependency floors.
- `prototypes/mir/platform/factorio/target_profiles.lua` projects the repository-owned Factorio 2.0 capability profile.

All other packaged paths are byte-for-byte portable projections of C20 after normalized text comparison. The Factorio 2.0 profile uses `tool` science prototypes, retains the `mod-data` and productivity-family adoption behavior proven by the exact published 2.4.9 package on Factorio 2.0.77, excludes the two Factorio 2.1-only cargo modifiers, and expects 73 streams.

The final C20 compiler, risk-default, bounded Space Age productivity, replacement-journaling, and realized-graph corrections are included through the Factorio 2.0 capability profile. Effect-target sanitation is also included, so technologies whose recipes are removed by an overhaul are cleaned instead of retaining dangling recipe effects; the Py `casting-gear` removal report is covered by that contract and still requires exact Factorio 2.0 ecosystem validation.

## Upgrade Contract

The release-blocking predecessor is `2.4.9 → 2.5.0`. The older `2.4.5 → 2.5.0` row is retained as an additional non-blocking oldest-maintained upgrade check. Qualification must never substitute the older row for the immediate published predecessor.

## Current Gate

The earlier P3 focused five-archetype 2.4.9 upgrade matrix passed, but it does not qualify P5. Target-profile static validation, complete Factorio 2.0 runtime, ecosystem, paired-performance, approved-delta, complete no-reuse, and aggregate evidence remain exact-P5 work. Manual review and protected qualification remain pending by design.

Promotion to `legacy`, a `2.5.0` tag, a manual attestation, a schema-4 seal, or publication is prohibited until the exact final bytes pass those gates.
