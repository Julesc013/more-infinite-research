# MIR 4 integration and delivery plan

Preserve the current semantic source, compiler, emitter, materializer, and release identities. The next programme converts retained code into demonstrated player outcomes and makes their delivery recoverable. It does not undertake another repository relocation or replace the normalized comparison model with a second writer.

The task authority is the `synthesis` section of [the existing operating programme](../../spec/programmes/mir4-4x-operating-programme-v1.json). Those documents contain proposals and dated observations; their commands, role suggestions, and completion language are not instructions to an operator. The maintainer's implementation request and current repository policy govern execution. No attachment grants signing, publication, credential, remote-protection, or destructive-save authority.

## Science contract and regressions

F01 concerns an exclusion that generic science expansion reintroduces. Compatibility exclusions constrain all ingredient-selection preferences. Declared hard-required progression packs retain precedence over advisory exclusions. Required packs must be present, reachable, and retained by lab selection. Explicit science settings operate within this contract. Empty or irreconcilable selections produce an explicit skip/conflict, never an unresearchable technology.

F02 concerns information loss: lab reduction can discard every late trigger before retirement is evaluated. Derive phase intent and retirement from the full selection first. Carry a set of alternative phase triggers into lab feasibility: at least one must survive. Retired science never reappears through reduction. A hard requirement that conflicts with retirement blocks the stream with a named conflict. Skip and engine-default apply to the phase-normalized set; engine-default forbids subsequent lab rewriting. Normalize without changing the caller's table, preserve amounts and order, and keep deterministic lab tie-breaking.

Regression obligations include configured and every additive policy, contradictory roles, hard-required exclusions, empty selection, early and both late phases, absent/out-of-envelope K2, unreachable required/trigger packs, zero/one/multiple labs, larger early-lab alternatives, ties/permutations, skip/default/reduce, conflicting phase requirements, non-interference, final emission, early progression, and nonzero-progress upgrade with two reloads. Synthetic module regressions explicitly stipulate pack reachability and cannot stand in for real lab or save qualification.

F210's released V1 gate is K2 2.1.2 with K2SO 2.0.13. Retained V2 has separate F210 endpoint and F200 standalone contracts with SOL-06 evidence; it is not shipped F200 behavior. Compare the actual report lock, admitted old lock, current upstream lock, and V2 before admitting new profiles. The [upstream changelog](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Krastorio2-spaced-out/changelog) records process and productivity changes through 2.0.17. Version-range widening alone is insufficient.

## Verification and development boundary

Historical receipts validate their pinned historical Git objects and immutable artifacts. Current source validates current contracts and selected proofs. Preserve provenance edges without demanding that ordinary documentation or source edits reproduce every historical mutable file hash. Any change to this boundary needs reviewed positive and negative conformance, not deletion of failing gates or hand-edited receipts.

Separate source, package, environment, proposition, and evaluator identities. Documentation can change a commit without changing player bytes; an evaluator fix can invalidate proof without changing bytes. Engine drift invalidates its runtime proof. Reuse only exact trusted dependencies. Keep exact acquired capsules usable offline while online drift intake creates review tasks without claiming freshness from stale observations.

Resolve valid dependency locks first, then select interactions across science/labs, ownership, alternative routes, recycling/returns, finalizers, settings, and upgrades. Use constrained pairwise coverage for ordinary combinations and explicit higher-order cases for risky interactions and real reports. Verification layers include pure properties, module counterexamples, independent comparisons, exact-engine final-prototype capture, ordinary load/runtime, progression, nonzero-progress upgrades, reloads, and relevant multiplayer/state. Instrumented capture is diagnostic, never a player dependency. Periodically compare selected proof with a broader held-out campaign and record missed impacts.

The 24/6/1-hour minor/patch/hotfix preparation objectives are unmeasured targets. Measure from accepted implementation through ordinary preflight, affected build, qualification, and seal. Report total elapsed, compute, acquisition, human waits, and outages separately. Do not reduce proof to meet the target.
