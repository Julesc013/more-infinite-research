# MIR 1.9.5 Factorio 1.1 Local Candidate Record

MIR 1.9.5 projects the unified fixed, linear, exponential, and hybrid research-cost semantics to Factorio 1.1. Its historical target base remains the qualified development anchor `53090c6f860bf9cb9aaf10140cbfe3924ef59c6c`; the added portable cost source is the verified pre-wave MIR 3.2.5 development authority `1281ac96a3991d0e26cd298c5d40492a47b588f6`.

The target uses Factorio 1.1 `count` and `count_formula`, the legacy `global` runtime store, and its reduced settings surface. Modern `mod-data` compatibility reports, generalized support transport, Space Age behavior, recipe-productivity generation, and native-owner adoption remain omitted by capability. This is a target-local candidate only while final MIR 3.2.5 lineage and outage publication authority remain incomplete.

Qualification requires the exact Factorio 1.1.110 binary, deterministic double construction, exact-ZIP load, a direct 1.9.4 upgrade, static/package gates, and the target research-cost model suite. Local evidence is written under `build/reports/release/1.9.5`; release ZIPs are not placed in `dist/` until the complete local outage seal is valid.
