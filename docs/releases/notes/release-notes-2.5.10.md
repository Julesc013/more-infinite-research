# More Infinite Research 2.5.10

This emergency hotfix brings the absolute maximum-level contract to Factorio 2.0.77. It preserves completed research above a newly lowered cap, blocks invalid current or queued research, and restores progression when the cap is raised or returned to infinite.

The package was qualified against exact Factorio 2.0.77, sealed as candidate `2.5-P14`, published as immutable tag `2.5.10`, and independently redownloaded from GitHub with matching bytes. MIR 2.5.9 remains unchanged and downloadable as the historical predecessor.
