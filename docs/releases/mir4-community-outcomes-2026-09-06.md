# MIR 4.2 community implementation outcomes

This development increment implements the bundled modern research browser, guarded finite material declarations, and package-excluded optional prototypes. The 4.2 mission remains open. It is **not a release candidate**, a broad overhaul support claim, or an authorization to publish.

The [existing operating programme](../../spec/programmes/mir4-4x-operating-programme-v1.json) owns execution. Its [active request authority](../../spec/programmes/community-requests.json) is an 81-request ledger; its intake and material/science origins remain traceable within that single authority rather than being a current 66-plus-23 count. The supplied assessment is proposal and attribution; the [measured evidence](../../spec/programmes/evidence/community-2026-09-06/outcomes.json) records actual implementation outcomes. PR #254 was closed without merging; the existing science fix on dev is the source authority.

## Bundled player code

F210 and F200 contain one MIR Research Browser, opened by its top button or `/mir-research`. Personal All/Finite/Infinite, status, family and literal ID search filters never assign force technology state. The native queue is mirrored with ten entries and an explicit remainder count. Enqueue checks force membership, prerequisites, permissions and duplicates before calling the native API. Technology details show actual ingredients and effects. The separate Inspector still supplies detailed route omission explanations.

The settings tab groups canonical settings by technology. Startup values include the effective MIR profile and can be exported through the existing validated MIRSET1 import path. Only owned runtime Boolean settings can apply live; runtime-global edits require an administrator. The current MIR settings are startup settings.

Catalogues are limited to 30,000 technologies, pages and effect groups to 20 rows, and search strings to 160 characters. The browser has no idle tick polling. F110 and F100 explicitly omit its modern modules. Native single-player GUI and partial-research save/reload captures cover the modern engines; two-client multiplayer, configuration-change and removal acceptance remain open.

## Exact material outcomes

Every declaration has a stable new manifest identity and uses the existing matching, ownership, science planning and emitter path. Defaults are three 2% tiers; this is not the requested later infinite continuation. Tin and bronze are available with red science in the captured Bob environment. Other streams follow their exact unlock-derived frontier and report feasible labs. Published identities remain unchanged.

The route guard requires explicit productivity permission and fails closed when it finds any potential recipe-graph return path from a candidate output to an input, including at traversal-budget limits. The only narrow exception is an exact profile-locked reviewed-forward certificate for the named final recipe: it must prove one bounded ordinary deterministic production route with the required fact, risk fingerprint, and complete mod closure. The current certificates cover only the three bounded Rare Metals, Silicon, and Glass outcomes below. Oxygen, water, recycling, matter, weighted, or arbitrary scripted routes otherwise remain rejected; the guard neither proves an exploit nor certifies them safe.

## Optional prototypes

The optional Car and maintenance implementations live under `tests/runtime/` and are excluded from all player packages. Each modern engine passed 278 probe assertions. Paired vanilla cars travelled 76.828125 tiles at matching speed while the economy car consumed 0.8 times the control energy. Clean removal restores captured setters; conflicts preserve another writer's changed value and withdraw uncontested MIR contributions. This does not qualify locomotives, spiders, tanks or every modded Car.

Maintenance is off by default in the prototype. Damage admission accepts walls/gates, caps the queue at 256, processes at most eight entries per step and waits 600 ticks since the latest damage. Disable, invalid entities and force changes remove pending work. The scheduler unregisters when the queue becomes empty. Player service admission, save migration, multiplayer, clone/quality and surface lifecycle proof remain open.

The programme retains later infinite continuation, Angel-only and combined material routes, weighted process proofs for withheld routes, affected-save upgrades and two reloads, browser multiplayer/removal acceptance, ecosystem canaries and balance as unfinished work.
