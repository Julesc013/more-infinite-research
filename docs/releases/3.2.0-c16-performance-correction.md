# MIR 3.2.0 C16 Performance Correction

## Trust-boundary correction

Records entering from external data, serialized artifacts, provider input, or actual post-mutation output still receive complete validation. Internally constructed immutable records are admitted once and then checked through module-private weak-key trust registries and stored fingerprints.

The optimized compiler:

- separates untrusted verification from cheap trusted assertions;
- shares immutable gates, designs, qualifications, candidates, and catalog children instead of repeatedly copying them;
- reuses admitted fingerprints and fixed semantic comparisons;
- retains read-only compiler, family, stream, and graph views;
- avoids deep catalog publication during ordinary compilation;
- preserves strict final graph, operation, mutation, sanitation, and prototype postconditions;
- retains tamper and copied-record rejection fixtures.

Canonical fingerprint semantics did not change.

## Remaining gates

C16 is not release-qualified. It still requires:

- the complete exact no-reuse automated plan under the frozen C16 qualification contract;
- exact 3.1.9 approved-delta, upgrade, ecosystem, and performance evidence from that final plan;
- maintainer gameplay and presentation review;
- schema-2 manual attestation;
- protected no-reuse qualification;
- schema-4 seal and independent seal check;
- promotion, tag, publication, and downloaded-byte verification.

Any gameplay, technology, recipe, balance, settings, locale, migration, asset, or packaged-document change creates C17. Qualification-only evidence and governance changes remain C16 while package roots reproduce the identity above.
