# MIR 2.5.0 P10 Playtest Guide

Use only candidate 2.5-P10, built from baseline 7ebe93029695bbf809a15a14c6540530738a9e62, portable C30 source c1fd8b932c8d916a14925678056e08893b87b2db, and Factorio 2.0 package source 39f2238c4f83790bf0d2bf5f1459503e96c41925. Require archive SHA-256 4BF7DB4238437A3C2C93F08DAEA30C4376C5222C8017C8A63F959C30264DD589 before testing.

## Order

1. Base startup, technology tree, settings, save, and reload.
2. Official Factorio 2.0 Space Age profile and concrete planet-discovery effects.
3. Ice and Platform progression: cryogenic science, sole recipe ownership, plus 10 percent ice-platform and plus 5 percent space-platform-foundation effects.
4. Structural logistics with opaque belt and inserter names, disabled automatic productivity, generic module tiers, and native AdvancedBeltsSA 2.3.3.
5. A copied MIR 2.4.9 save, preserving research, progress, settings, generated identities, and explicit new Platform ownership.
6. The exact Factorio 2.0 Py closure, proving casting-gear remains present and casting-mk02 retains valid ordered effects without sanitation.
7. Maintained 2.0 ecosystem profiles, then large compatible stress profiles.

## Acceptance

- Platform Productivity exists exactly once only with Space Age.
- Ice and Platform both require cryogenic science; Platform does not require metallurgic science.
- Landfill no longer owns ice-platform or space-platform-foundation.
- Structural fallback covers the governed belt and inserter types without overriding explicit productivity denial.
- AdvancedBeltsSA 2.3.3 reaches its exact native Factorio 2.0 assertions.
- Existing Landfill and Ice research are retained on upgrade; Platform begins unresearched.
- Py finalizer ordering retains all valid present targets and removes only genuinely stale targets.

## Release boundary

Record observations against exact P10. Any package-visible correction creates P11. Automated evidence, the seven-item manual attestation, protected qualification, and the seal must all bind the same archive and package source.
