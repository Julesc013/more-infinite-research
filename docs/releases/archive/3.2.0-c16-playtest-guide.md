# MIR 3.2.0 C16 In-Game Playtest Guide

Use only the exact C16 archive:

```text
dist/more-infinite-research_3.2.0.zip
SHA-256 4646277AC8FBC67D453EAAAEE13C3167630AD94BFE490AD08D592844B6D7B38D
bytes 1,014,593
entries 288
Factorio 2.1.11
```

C16 changes compiler performance internals, not technology behavior. Its seven governed semantic exports are byte-identical to C15, and the exact K2SO and BZ closures passed the focused paired campaign. Manual findings must nevertheless bind C16 because C15 evidence cannot attest different ZIP bytes.

Use separate user-data directories for Base, Space Age, K2SO, and large packs. Do not run performance qualification while playing or while another Factorio process, backup, scanner, download, or benchmark is active.

## Modes

Run each representative ecosystem first in safe mode:

- safe attachments enabled;
- automatic creation disabled;
- reviewed-data requirement enabled.

Then use preview mode:

- preview action;
- new candidates discoverable;
- full catalog export enabled;
- no prototype mutation from unapproved candidates.

Use experimental creation only in a disposable save after inspecting the preview catalog. Do not turn every discovered proposal into a promotion.

## Review order

### 1. Base and 3.1.9 upgrade

Confirm exactly one MIR Steel technology, real forward-production effects, reachable prerequisites, producible science, accepting labs, sensible cost and effect, retained research levels, retained fractional progress, unchanged startup settings, and successful save/reload.

No unexpected generic technology should appear when automatic creation is off.

### 2. Space Age

Confirm vanilla steel productivity is the sole owner and covers smelting and casting exactly once. Check plastic, processing units, low-density structures, and rocket fuel for one owner each. Native-owner costs and effects must remain unchanged unless the corresponding explicit setting is enabled.

### 3. K2SO

Load `Krastorio2-spaced-out` and `xy-k2so-enhancements-nulls-fork` with Space Age. Open:

```text
recipe-prod-research_advanced_circuit-1
```

It must contain:

```text
automation-science-pack
logistic-science-pack
chemical-science-pack
production-science-pack
electromagnetic-science-pack
```

Red-only science is a release blocker. Sample early, middle, and late technologies for accepting labs, reachable prerequisites, correct ownership, and appropriate K2SO progression. Matter, recycling, voiding, and broad transmutation families remain unapproved.

### 4. Focused overhaul rings

Review AAI, BZ, Bob, the planet cluster, and the resource mega ring separately. Use a mega-smash profile only as a scale and startup canary. Reproduce any failure in the smallest isolation ring before changing MIR.

### 5. Presentation and lifecycle

Complete the formal manual rows for:

- technology-tree layout and prerequisite arrows;
- icons and fallbacks;
- locale fit, truncation, and RTL rendering;
- settings UX;
- save UI and partial research;
- representative balance;
- connected-player `give-item` configuration-change safety;
- mod removal, re-addition, and settings changes for any proposed promotion.

## Candidate review record

For each proposed promotion record:

1. exact candidate, design, and qualification fingerprints;
2. exact mod versions, settings, and load order;
3. included recipes and excluded near-matches;
4. owner before and after;
5. science, labs, prerequisites, and progression span;
6. cost and effect at levels 1, 5, 10, and 20;
7. upgrade, removal, and re-addition behavior;
8. applicability envelope and migration decision;
9. explicit human approval and promotion admission.

Prefer existing-stream attachment, then exact owner cooperation, then one exact reviewed technology, and only then a structural-envelope technology. Recovery, recycling, catalyst, biology, broad chemistry, and ambiguous material families remain preview-only until their stricter evidence is complete.

## Candidate consequences

If C16 is accepted unchanged, complete its manual attestation and protected qualification without rebuilding the ZIP.

Any package-visible tuning, promotion, recipe, science, prerequisite, cost, effect, ownership, settings, locale, migration, asset, or packaged-document change creates C17. Record optional preferences for 3.2.1 or 3.3 when they are not release blockers.
