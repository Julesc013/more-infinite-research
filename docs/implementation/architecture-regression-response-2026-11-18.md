# Architecture and regression response

The inherited-5.0.11 worker reader does not protect every research system, historical version or mod combination. It proves one historical admission and reuses the existing transfer implementation. The broader defect is inconsistent enforcement of shared obligations: origin versus current version, optional absence versus a failed observation, and retained bookkeeping versus preserved native effects. Those inconsistencies require correction across their real consumers.

Retain MIR 5's authored native construction, canonical source, small Generator and edition packaging. Repair and restructure the migration, lifecycle and test-support boundaries. The available evidence does not justify restarting the whole product. A rewrite would still have to preserve existing identities, paid research, partial work, queues and old ownership; it would introduce another migration boundary without recovering facts already erased from a save.

### First outcomes and decision gate

The first product milestone is one diagnosed failure mechanism, an independently specified regression that rejects the faulty behavior, and a repaired player operation preserved through the applicable successor save and two cold loads. A diagnostic improvement, protective refusal, prepared controller or host pass is recorded as that narrower outcome. None completes a supported upgrade or closes an unreproduced reporter case.

Start with the current worker rejection: identify the failed route, creator, work-contract or flag predicate and retain bounded expected/observed differences and validation causes. Trace the relationship from authored construction through later processing to final engine prototypes and runtime admission. Do not prescribe historical preparation for an invalid current definition. Preserve existing saved fingerprint readers; a new comparison cannot silently reinterpret old stored fingerprints.

Use a 60-minute diagnostic checkpoint to state what was learned, the discriminating next observation and remaining uncertainty. After two unsuccessful evidence-backed hypothesis cycles, review the design before another cycle. Replace a boundary when its present roles cannot express the preservation contract coherently, or when the proposed repair requires contradictory authorities, guessed historical work or proliferating scenario exceptions. A missing archive, unavailable original save, fixture error or resource refusal is an input/execution problem rather than proof that the player architecture must be replaced. Checkpoints control investigation cost; they do not promise a repair duration or permit relaxed acceptance.
