# MIR 4 ProcessIR preview

ProcessIR normalizes exact recipe/process observations for comparison and safety analysis. It preserves terminal fact authority and represents incomplete knowledge as `UNKNOWN`.

The MIR 4.0 preview includes exact F210/F200 captures, target-bound snapshots, deterministic Inspector comparison, and compatibility canary evidence. Those artifacts prove the recorded propositions only. They do not authorize broad automatic synthesis or replace the player planner and emitter.

When adding a capture, bind the exact environment lock, retain catalyst/probability/quality/loop semantics, validate bounds, and compare repeated runs before making a claim.
